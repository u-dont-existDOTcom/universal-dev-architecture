# Instruction composition and portable intelligence

Status: candidate implementation of the owner-approved 2026-09-12 refactor.
Scope: instruction authors, maintainers, and active-contract composition; not an extra checklist for every answer.

## Ownership and reachability

Maintain one normative owner per rule and scope. A consumer inherits the applicable rule and records only its local trigger, binding, or stricter requirement. A repository name, link, or import declaration is not evidence that its instruction text reached the actor: preserve the actual load/injection route. Follow the current owner's bootstrap cadence, including a live root read on every user turn when required. Bind exact task sources and reconcile changed authority instead of silently mixing revisions; a pinned data/method baseline does not become editable merely because governance was refreshed.

Use these layers:

- Universal development architecture: portable development and reasoning controls.
- Mission Control: a Universal-governed operational subsystem; only coordination, admission, routing, state, and recovery specializations.
- AskRigor internal development: Universal plus applicable Mission Control supervision, followed by AskRigor engineering and protocol-maintenance deltas.
- AskRigor public Universal Instructions and HRP: complete public protocols with only their declared public dependencies. Their users do not inherit the maintainers' development bootstrap, personal preferences, host configuration, private supervisor directory, or internal spending/transport policy.

Development governance governs how a public artifact is changed. It does not automatically become a runtime dependency of that artifact. Operational authority and scientific authority are different domains: HRP does not authorize deployment or spending; a successful orchestration gate does not establish scientific adequacy.

## Composition, not accumulation

Read the applicable entry point and its authority chain; use the index as a selector, not as an instruction to load every listed pattern. Within one turn's already-authorized chain, load each canonical rule once; this does not waive a required fresh root read on the next user turn. Local instructions add only the actual difference and its enforcement binding. Keep current owner/project requirements, privacy boundaries, full-protocol obligations, and mandatory specialist modules intact. A more specific applicable requirement controls its domain; do not use file order to silently erase a protected requirement. Escalate a genuinely unresolved material conflict through the authorized reasoning route.

During instruction maintenance, classify repeated text as one of: redundant active copy; necessary self-contained public distribution; purposeful boundary reminder; historical provenance; or distinct rule with a similar surface form. Do not delete by lexical similarity alone. Full-protocol source preservation is not permission to replace the complete protocol with an excerpt. Separate historical explanations from operative rules, but move or remove canonical protocol history only in an explicitly reviewed protocol migration.

A short reminder at the exact action or delivery boundary can be justified even when the full rule has already been loaded. Reference the same rule identity and preserve its scope; do not maintain a second divergent policy. The owner-validated final-output timestamp mechanism remains protected. The owner's later correction explicitly expands it from the first turn to every turn; this is not an invitation to remove the intermediate-output exclusion or final-output check.

## Transfer decisions

Promote only the portable causal or operational lesson, with its originating source, evidence, scope, limits, and non-transfer cases. Project-specific examples and provider capabilities remain local/version-bound. Candidate lessons are not active policy merely because they were discovered. Downstream specializations must not create an upstream runtime dependency or a circular bootstrap.

Public packaging may include a self-contained adaptation of a portable lesson. This is intentional distribution duplication, not a second internal runtime import. Record its lineage in a development-side transfer ledger. Do not put private locators, account-specific permissions, private data, or mandatory internal-repository reads into the public protocol.

## Preservation and verification

For each change map the old obligation to its retained normative owner, applicable consumer, and enforcement boundary. Retain exceptions, non-substitutes, safety conditions, and incomplete/unavailable states. Test the literal answer, artifact, directive, request, or installed source where the effect is required; the active-contract lifecycle is owned by patterns/task-time-lesson-activation.md, not duplicated here.

Measure native instruction-chain bytes, explicitly loaded guidance, duplicate operative clauses, and the relevant interface's real limits separately. File size is a diagnostic, not a quality score. A successful source edit, a passed static check, an accepted owner test, and a current deployment/package are separate evidence classes. Do not report universal live compliance from text-presence tests.

When a transfer or deduplication cannot be verified, preserve the original protection and record the precise unresolved item. This is not permission to certify the rest of an uninspected corpus.
