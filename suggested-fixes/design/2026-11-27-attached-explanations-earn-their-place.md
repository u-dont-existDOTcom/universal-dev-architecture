## What to do

In the method's content guidance: text that rides along with an item (a card's "Why this card?", a caption, a note under a heading, a tooltip) earns its place the way the item does. Keep it only for context the reader needs to follow the item, such as a term or reference the item uses, or for safety wording. A line that says why the topic matters, what the source thinks or how the item came about goes, and an item left without one shows no control for it.

## Why

Every card in the game carried a "why", and a review of 204 texts had checked each one against its source but never asked whether a player needed it. The owner asked for them all to go unless they gave necessary context; 5 of 139 did (two safety lines and three that explain a phrase their question uses).
