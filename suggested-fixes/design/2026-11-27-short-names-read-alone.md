## What to do

In the method's guidance on interface wording: a label, button name or heading is read alone, before any explanation, for what it says about the person who chooses it. Read each one that way before it ships, and show its explanation under the name wherever the name alone could be taken another way.

## Why

"I need to watch what I do" was written beside its explanation ("Thinking won't settle this. What I do in everyday life will show me."), where it is clear. A player sees the name first, and alone it says something about them: to the owner, that he was afraid to do what he really wanted. It became "Life will show me", and every label in the game now shows its explanation under its name.

## Check first

Whether the method's wording or component guidance already asks for names to be read without their descriptions.
