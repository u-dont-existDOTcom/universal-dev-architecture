## What to do

1. **Map:** Amend the existing `ROUTE.RELATIONAL_REALITY_CHECK` following the already covered bidirectional chronology assessment. After enough facts are known, distinguish (a) the person's goal in communicating, (b) the reaction they realistically anticipated at the time, including surprise or foreseeable tradeoffs, and (c) how they evaluate both their manner and the resulting relationship now. Compare all three before selecting a next step. The prior rule about defining a realistic *future* outcome does not cover reviewing an *already completed* encounter.
2. **Therapy rules:** Do not ask only what a person hoped would happen when accessible context strongly suggests an answer and the more discriminating information is whether they anticipated the other person's reaction and how they now evaluate their choice. A person may feel justified in the stance, regret the wording, and grieve the lost relationship at once; these are compatible. Do not assume regret, satisfaction, desire for reconciliation, fault, or fixed character. Once the sequence is sufficiently known, stop requesting more incident detail unless the missing detail could change action or safety.
3. **Question selection:** Closely related questions can form one coherent high-information inquiry when they investigate the same decision. Balance answer value against cognitive load rather than counting punctuation mechanically. Preserve the one-primary-next-move discipline and defer low-value subsidiary questions when needed. Use the existing critique, focus and response-fidelity channels; do not add a new mandatory model evaluator without evidence.
4. **Guide propagation:** Consider a concise reader-facing teaching point: *After a painful disagreement, ask what you were trying to achieve, what response you expected, and how you now feel about what you said and what followed. You can stand by your convictions and still wish you had expressed them differently; you may also accept an unavoidable consequence without regretting your choice.* Compare with current differentiation, relational reality-check, draft editing and repair sections. If truly novel and owner-approved, update the canonical AI guide and humanized-guide queue in the same reviewed change; otherwise record an explicit app-only or already-covered disposition.
5. **Regressions:** Synthetic tests should distinguish unexpected rejection from knowingly accepted distance, regret about tone from regret about beliefs, and mixed feelings. Tests should verify an adaptive next step (communication repair, boundary, relational fit, or optional developmental work), not merely match rule text. Preserve safety and accountability asymmetries where coercion or threats are supported.

## Why

An initial question about the communicative goal was plausible but might simply restate an obvious hope to be heard. The supervising owner identified a more useful discriminator: whether the response was surprising, foreseeable in hindsight, and judged differently now. This expands the current relational reality check beyond reconstructing actions to understanding the person's own assessment of the consequence. It must not become a requirement to interrogate all three dimensions in every routine encounter.
