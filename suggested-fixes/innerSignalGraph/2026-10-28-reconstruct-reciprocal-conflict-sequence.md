## What to do

1. **Map:** Review `ROUTE.RELATIONAL_REALITY_CHECK` and `IC.DIFFERENTIATION`. Where someone describes wanting acceptance, then confronting another person and experiencing rejection, recover the *missing middle*: what was first said and done, what the speaker said and did, the counterpart's words and actions, timing and boundaries, and how the result was interpreted. When sequence is missing, ask one specific, non-accusatory question before deciding who caused the rupture or whether an old developmental pattern explains it. Do not repeat an already answered question.
2. **Rules and review:** Distinguish observations, emotions, beliefs about motives, and justifications. A person saying they seek love does not demonstrate that their confrontation was loving; another person's discomfort does not prove that person was coercive or rejecting beliefs. Possible patterns include genuine exclusion, a legitimate boundary in response to confrontational behavior, mixed escalation, and unknown chronology. Retain separate safety/accountability distinctions when threats or intimidation are actually supported. Do not equate anger with hatred or current harmful intent. Add this to existing fidelity/critic and focus rules rather than requiring an extra generic model evaluator.
3. **AI guide and humanized guide:** Evaluate one concise reader teaching point: *Before deciding that someone's reaction to a disagreement proves they are unloving, notice what each person actually said and did. You can hold your convictions and boundaries while learning to disagree with respect and without assuming motives.* Explain the novel reader need versus current guide text; if approved, update the canonical AI guide and add the related humanized-guide teaching point to `authoring/PENDING-PUBLIC-GUIDE-CHANGES.md` in the same reviewed change. Avoid a lecture that implies the more powerful or threatening party and the threatened party are equally responsible.
4. **Tests:** Use synthetic scenes where rejection occurred before any confrontation, where an unnecessarily hostile approach prompts a reasonable boundary, where both escalate, and where the sequence is unknown. Require a differentiated interpretation and a question whose plausible answers affect whether to use protective action, accountability/communication work, or developmental integration. Never force childhood imagery.
5. **Delivery:** Preserve actually sent replies versus superseded unsent drafts. A supervisor correction updates an unsent master rather than prompting an additional client reply.

## Why

A supervisor detected that an assistant jumped from a reported value and self-described anger directly to interpreting anger, even though the dialogue and behavior between disagreement, confrontation and rejection were missing. Reconstructing that sequence first is more likely to reveal a useful next step without assuming guilt, victimhood, or hidden motives. This is a strengthened application of existing relational reality-check and focus rules, not a new therapy method.
