## What to do

Create a new, provisional **Practitioner and Community Discovery** lane under the broad Natural Treatment Atlas mission. This is a **lead-discovery layer, not an efficacy-certification layer**. Do not claim this lane exists in an Atlas database unless actual authorized ingestion has been verified.

1. **Source discovery.** Begin with public, legally accessible articles and indices of `endsickness.org`, `midwesterndoctor.com` (The Forgotten Side of Medicine, hosted by Substack), and `orthomolecular.org/resources/omns/` (Orthomolecular Medicine News Service). Find original case series, clinician case reports, annotated references, outcomes and disagreement, including missed negative results and historical/citation-lineage sources. Expand to other practitioners by *independently testable high-value leads*, not by ideological similarity or sensational adjectives. Record source/author/date/archived or updated version, publication type, and access restrictions.
2. **Claim-level extraction, not unverified 'best' lists.** One evidence record per condition/phenotype × exact intervention/formulation/route × outcome. Record the source author's actual claim, the basis (practitioner case record, patient report, original trial, synthesis, citation-only, or mechanism), timing, comparator, co-interventions, count exposed/count benefiting if known, quantified patient-centered outcome, negative reports, adverse effects, preparation reproducibility and exact source locator. Separate the author's interpretation from observed outcomes. A single case in a protocol with many simultaneous interventions cannot identify which component was causal.
3. **Commercial/dependency audit.** Check supplements and practitioner referral/affiliate/reseller relationships, sample selection, incentives to post positive testimonials, author-response amplification, duplication across newsletters/forums, and whether a marketed formula matches its cited studies. Avoid censorship-by-affiliation: conflicts affect uncertainty, not permission to inspect.
4. **Independent evidence bridge.** Translate promising hypotheses into matched formal source searches and full-text audits, alongside counterevidence at the exact route/condition; do not repeat favorable case reports as independent observations when they share patients, authors or readers. Preserve genuinely underdocumented high-effect candidates rather than forcing a binary effective/ineffective classification from lack of RCTs.
5. **Forum and comment evidence.** Preserve distinct *firsthand exposure*, independent corroboration, explicit no-response, adverse outcomes, diagnosis/exposure uncertainty and concurrent treatments. Score expected observability and corpus selection bias. Do **not** turn selected comment ratios into efficacy rates.
6. **Permission/license gate before bulk Substack ingestion.** A single bounded test on a publicly viewable Midwestern Doctor thread found that a Bright Data call could retrieve a response containing 3,711 unique comment objects while the public page displayed ~3,750 comments; a normal HTML comments page exposed far fewer. This proves a technical extraction route, **not completeness or permission**. Substack Terms of Use (updated 2026-10-06) expressly restrict automated scraping/copying substantial Substack content; its Developer API Terms authorize narrow public creator/profile data, not an unlimited research-comments license. Do not start/continue bulk collection through Bright Data, the undocumented comment endpoint or browser automation unless the relevant rights/access permissions and source terms authorize it. Preferred route: publisher-provided comment export, written permission, or a genuinely authorized API/service; otherwise use brief permitted excerpts and manual source-level discovery. Do not bypass logins, paywalls, rate controls, or reproduce or publicly redistribute commenter-level text, medical history or identifiers. Keep any licensed source data in access-controlled storage; public Atlas entries are deidentified syntheses with publication links and provenance.
7. **Correct Deep Research intake.** On the current product surface, a Deep Research user may receive only its **rendered result/report** and no portable ZIP, JSONL, or individually downloadable evidence files. Accept that actual complete result as the input; extract a structured ledger **locally in AskRigor/Chat after receipt**, preserving every original claim/citation and explicit unknowns. Never demand that the user attach files that Deep Research cannot deliver, never invent sibling artifacts or sandbox paths, and never fail an otherwise useful study pass solely for lack of ZIP output. If the result is a short summary rather than full evidence, classify the actual coverage as PARTIAL and seek source expansion, not fabricated records. Preserve topic-fidelity: a research-method discussion does not satisfy a disease-specific treatment evidence assignment.
8. **Atlas progression.** For each candidate assign `new_lead / source_only / original_human_evidence / independent_consistent / contradicted / unsafe / unresolved` (multi-axis when appropriate) and `accessible_full_original / partial / abstract_only / secondhand / blocked`. Rank candidate follow-up by expected usefulness, outcome magnitude, severity, availability, safety and cost of verification. Keep a prioritized full-study/book/monograph acquisition queue, negative search receipts and a per-topic marginal-yield checkpoint. Do not mistake a weak single-platform evidence result for global source saturation.
9. **Three distinct pilot screens**, none a treatment endorsement:
   - EndSickness: separate its mold/MCAS and gut/food-symptom protocols into individual source-supported components (e.g. environmental exposure mitigation, symptom-trigger logging, paced breathing, prebiotic formulation, and binders). Article authors sometimes cite family/client experience and disclose supplement reseller interests. No denominator or matched counterfactual for multi-component 'cures' is available from those article narratives. Investigate potential harms and independently measured claims, not just positive summaries.
   - The Forgotten Side of Medicine: investigate the author's cited historical DMSO primary sources *and* independently measured DMSO-alone human outcomes versus source-matched positive/negative commenter reports. For example, the 2009 five-arm 775-person knee-osteoarthritis study (`PMID 19380203`) reported no difference between DMSO vehicle and placebo on its endpoints, whereas diclofenac-in-DMSO outperformed vehicle; trial abstracts inspected, full audit pending. That result is endpoint/formulation-specific and does not automatically refute every other DMSO use. The publication actively solicits favorable reports: comments are discovery and expected-observability traces, not an unselected response cohort.
   - Orthomolecular Medicine News Service: treat essays as maps to forgotten primary publications, clinician records and hypotheses. Separate their own assertions from the cited trials and controls, search contradicting studies and adverse outcomes, and avoid recycling one advocacy essay as a second independent human cohort.
10. **Privacy/portability.** Public UDA/AskRigor suggestion files contain no owner personal health history, source commenters' identities, patient descriptions or raw comments. Derive generic examples solely from public scientific design/source metadata. Do not quote or replicate sensitive health experiences from the active chat.

## Why

The Atlas's highest-value clinical hypotheses may emerge first from regional practitioners, patient groups, heterogeneous symptom clusters and nonindexed clinician archives, rather than indexed trials. The three specified public source ecosystems contain materially different evidence functions: a condition/protocol-focused practitioner catalogue, a very large reader-comment community and historical-study advocacy archives. That breadth is useful when source dependence, permission and clinical appraisal are kept visible.

A preceding research workflow wrongly demanded separate ZIP or JSONL exports from Deep Research in a product surface that delivered only the research result. That turned a platform-format expectation into an artificial blocker and forced unnecessary owner file handling. Correct acceptance is **rendered result → evidence extraction by this research workflow → validated source/reasoning ledger**, not rendered result → impossible user-provided ZIP.

Unrestricted commentary scraping is also not a safe default merely because an endpoint can be accessed; publisher terms, copyright, personal health data and consent limit collection. Technical feasibility and authorized research use are distinct.
