# Do not let a generic probiotic study stand in for a specific histamine intervention

- For: `u-dont-existDOTcom/AskRigor`
- Filed: 2026-10-13, by ChatGPT Atlas research auditor
- From: source-based assessment of the Global Remedies Atlas practitioner/community report and original assignment; no private testimony submitted
- Owner request: yes; the owner explicitly requested bug reports for these defects
- Existing pull request: none
- Supersedes: none

## What to do

Require an exact intervention fingerprint for a trial before it can be proposed as evidence or given a final-summary slot: bacterial genera/species/strains where supplied, dose and formula, mono- versus multi-species, fermentation process, other ingredients, comparator, condition, and posted clinical outcomes. If a practitioner claim concerns a Bifidobacterium-only strategy or fermented bovine colostrum, a randomized trial of a different mixed Bifidobacterium/Lactobacillus commercial product is **adjacent work**, not a validation of the practitioner treatment. An unreported completed trial is not positive or negative efficacy evidence.

## Why

The published research report included the EndSickness Bifidobacterium-only and fermented-colostrum histamine-intolerance narrative, yet the final summary described the general category 'probiotics for histamine intolerance' and featured NCT07397962 as a top discovery. The primary ClinicalTrials.gov record identifies Probio Histamed: B. breve, B. lactis, B. infantis, B. longum, L. gasseri and L. rhamnosus, a six-species mixture, with 54 enrolled and no posted results as of 2026-10-13. It is not the practitioner's intervention, and trial completion is not observed symptom improvement.

## Check first

Check the canonical exact-product/heterodox evidence requirements and existing product-identity regression before modifying protocols. Regression: compare a practitioner testimonial for one fermented Bifidobacterium-only formulation with a completed 54-participant registration for a six-species blend that has no outcomes posted. Passing output identifies both separately, gives efficacy of the latter as unknown, and never treats it as direct verification of the former.
