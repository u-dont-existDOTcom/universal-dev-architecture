## Question

Why did the Life Patterns fixed-codebook detour survive despite existing universal owner-outcome, strategy, lesson-activation, and research-before-reinvention controls, and what is the smallest plausible universal repair?

This replay evaluates repository/control-path evidence. It does not claim access to historical hidden model reasoning.

## Finding 1 — durable activation/wiring was not established in Human Design

At the decisive ontology commit `5d40e6608ff7af3c8550d4eb5d746ccac6fa5088`, the checked-in Human Design `AGENTS.md` routed workers through project-local architecture/research files but did not route them to `universal-dev-architecture`, its lesson index, or an active lesson contract. Inspection of the historical project authority found no durable evidence that the relevant universal lessons were compiled into the Life Patterns task at that fork.

Therefore the existence of the universal policies did not reliably place them in the decision path. This is an activation/enforcement failure, not evidence that every semantic safeguard was absent from the model's general reasoning.

Implication: any universal repair must verify task-time activation on the actual reasoning path. Adding another stored rule without wiring would reproduce the same failure mode.

## Finding 2 — owner-outcome preservation alone does not expose a manufactured prerequisite

The participant-centered product outcome remained visibly present while the ontology was introduced as a prerequisite for later model comparison. A contract-to-owner comparison can therefore look superficially healthy:

- requested end product still named;
- theory-blind comparison still named;
- ontology described as enabling work rather than replacing the final product.

The failure is upstream of ordinary completion/proxy laundering: an assistant-created **method hypothesis** was promoted into a mandatory dependency without proving that the genuine constraint required that method.

A derived method can therefore consume substantial time while the literal owner outcome remains intact.

## Finding 3 — strategy-efficacy control was not sufficient at the initial fork

At the 17:35 architecture choice there was not yet a completed implementation cycle with direct product evidence showing regression. The outcome-advancement control is strongest after a strategy makes predictions or produces observable effects. It can help stop later schema accretion, especially once ordinary cases fail, but it is not the earliest control for an unjustified method commitment.

This argues for a pre-commitment method-necessity check, not replacement of the outcome-progress control.

## Finding 4 — research-before-reinvention could reinforce the wrong problem formulation

The research-before-reinvention gate correctly asks for the underlying problem, existing work, alternatives, and baselines. However, the current prior-work ledger did not require an explicit answer to:

> What breaks if the proposed method is removed or replaced, and what evidence rules out the strongest materially simpler alternative?

Once the project framed the task as building a neutral ontology/coder, literature on ontology engineering, psychometrics, codebooks, annotation, and inter-rater reliability could improve that solution without establishing that the solution was necessary for the owner outcome.

Therefore prior-art quality does not by itself protect against a misframed method commitment.

## Pilot candidate

Add one bounded section to the existing `templates/PRIOR-WORK-SCAN.md` rather than creating a parallel supervision framework:

- distinguish owner outcome, genuine constraint, and proposed method;
- record method origin (`owner_required`, `externally_required`, `assistant_hypothesis`, `inherited_project_choice`);
- ask what breaks if the method is removed/replaced;
- name the strongest materially simpler alternative;
- require evidence that rules out that alternative;
- show one ordinary case and one difficult counterexample;
- classify necessity as `ESTABLISHED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `NOT_NECESSARY`;
- allow unresolved methods only as bounded reversible experiments rather than hardened prerequisites.

The trigger is consequential method commitment, not routine implementation choice.

## Baseline vs candidate interpretation

### Existing-policy baseline

- **MP-001:** owner-outcome alignment may remain MATCH because the final product remains promised; prior-work scanning can occur inside the ontology framing. No existing template field forces explicit method necessity/removal analysis before architecture hardening. Result: plausible miss.
- **MP-002:** owner-outcome control should allow the requested taxonomy subject to ordinary research/implementation checks.
- **MP-003:** iteration-lane guidance should allow routine reversible work without owner interruption.
- **MP-004:** external hard constraint should be respected.

This is a policy-structure analysis, not an executed historical model benchmark.

### Candidate method-necessity check

The added fields make MP-001's unsupported inference explicit while leaving MP-002/MP-004 legitimate and MP-003 untriggered. That is the intended discrimination to test in a later fresh reasoning evaluation.

## What this pilot does NOT establish

- It does not prove the proposed fields will be reliably applied in every Chat/repository path.
- It does not prove a model will judge `necessity_state` correctly merely because the template contains the field.
- It does not establish that a new mandatory owner approval is needed at every architecture choice.
- It does not justify universal-main promotion yet.
- It does not claim that the Human Design incident was caused only by missing fields; activation/wiring was separately deficient.

## Required follow-up before promotion

1. Run a fresh reasoning evaluation over the four fixtures without supplying this audit's conclusions as the answer key in the evaluator prompt.
2. Add at least one unrelated real project decision and one adversarial case where the simpler-looking method actually violates a non-obvious hard constraint.
3. Measure false interruptions as well as catches.
4. Verify actual activation/wiring: the reasoning path must retrieve the relevant control before the consequential method commitment, not merely store it in GitHub.
5. If the main failure is wiring, fix wiring first. Promote the semantic template addition only if it adds discrimination beyond correctly activated existing controls.

## Owner-facing supervision implication

For consequential forks, the system—not the owner—should surface a compact statement:

1. **You asked for:** the outcome.
2. **I am additionally assuming:** the proposed method/prerequisite.
3. **Simpler live alternative:** the strongest materially simpler route.
4. **Why I think the added method is necessary:** decision-changing evidence, or explicitly `UNRESOLVED`.
5. **Next safe probe:** the smallest reversible test that can resolve the uncertainty.

Routine reversible work continues automatically. An unresolved assistant-inferred prerequisite should trigger a bounded probe, not silently become architecture and not automatically create an owner approval ceremony.

## Recommendation

Keep this change on the proposal branch. The evidence supports a two-part repair candidate:

- **wiring/activation:** ensure current universal controls actually reach the reasoning path;
- **semantic discrimination:** add a method-necessity/manufactured-prerequisite check at consequential method commitments.

Do not promote either as universally effective until the follow-up evaluation distinguishes improved detection from extra process burden.
