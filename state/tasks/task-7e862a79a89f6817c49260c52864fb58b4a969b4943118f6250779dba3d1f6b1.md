## Completed

- `tools/codex-mission-control/owner-runtime/supervisor_receipt_copier.py`: one throttled scan serves every waiting decision request (`decision_scan`). It runs at most once a minute. It reads threads updated after the earliest waiting request's floor: its send time at first, then the start of the last complete scan less 10 minutes (the provider clock skew Mission Control allows elsewhere). An incomplete or failed scan keeps the floors and doubles the wait, up to 15 minutes. Floors of requests no longer waiting are dropped. State lives in the copier's existing state file.
- `scripts/list-chatgpt-thread-final-messages.ts`: also reports whether the scan was complete (a thread list, no thread source reported unavailable, every read in range succeeded), with `sourcesUnavailable` and the count of failed reads. A lister that doesn't report it counts as incomplete.
- `tests/test_mission_control_supervisor_receipt_copier.py`: the throttle, the floors, the backoff and its cap, dropped floors, and one scan serving a cycle without scanning a published request again.
- Round 2, after Codex's review of `2cf61ba4` (two P1s): a request's floor moves only once the scan was evaluated for it and held no receipt for it, so a receipt found but not yet published (a failed readback record or GitHub write) stays visible to the next scan (`advance_decision_floors`); and a scan counts as complete only if the listing isn't cut by its 50-thread limit (fewer than 50 listed, or one updated before the floor), as the lister now reports (`truncated`).
- Round 3, after Codex's review of `c1d9b23e` (P1) and the hosted audit of `2cf61ba4`: a read counts only if it is this thread's (its id), with its turns and a status; any other shape, a thread without an id or time, or a temporary one counts as unreadable and leaves the scan incomplete. The task record's evidence lines carry the wording the repository's checks require.
- Round 4, after Codex's review of `a167af80` (P1; hosted checks green there): a short page alone no longer proves the listing complete; it must reach back past the floor (a ChatGPT or unknown-kind thread updated before it), as for the Work retry's search, since sources may be paged apart.
