## Completed

- `tools/codex-mission-control/owner-runtime/supervisor_receipt_copier.py`: one throttled scan serves every waiting decision request (`decision_scan`). It runs at most once a minute. It reads threads updated after the earliest waiting request's floor: its send time at first, then the start of the last complete scan less 5 minutes. An incomplete or failed scan keeps the floors and doubles the wait, up to 15 minutes. Floors of requests no longer waiting are dropped. State lives in the copier's existing state file.
- `scripts/list-chatgpt-thread-final-messages.ts`: also reports whether the scan was complete (a thread list, no thread source reported unavailable, every read in range succeeded), with `sourcesUnavailable` and the count of failed reads. A lister that doesn't report it counts as incomplete.
- `tests/test_mission_control_supervisor_receipt_copier.py`: the throttle, the floors, the backoff and its cap, dropped floors, and one scan serving a cycle without scanning a published request again.
