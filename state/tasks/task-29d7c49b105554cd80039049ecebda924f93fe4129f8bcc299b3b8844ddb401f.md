## Completed

- **`lib/owner-task-registration.ts`.**
  - Parses the four answers exactly: goal; may use, must never and done when, one item per line. Extra fields are refused; the passphrase is split off before parsing.
  - Records the owner source: canonical JSON of the answers and the form's standard lines, its SHA-256, `OWNER_REATTESTED`, an owner-session locator.
  - Derives the outcome, contract and reconciliation mechanically. Every text in them is an owner answer or a standard line the form shows.
  - A SYSTEM record sets the connection to `OFFLINE_CONFIGURED`.
  - A repeat records nothing. Other answers for a registered id are refused; an id with any records is refused, naming what is there.
- **`lib/owner-task-passphrase.ts`** (first review, P0). The daemon registers only with the owner's registration passphrase, checked against a scrypt verifier next to the database. No verifier means registration is off.
  - Wrong attempts are kept on disk: five in 15 minutes or twenty in a day lock it.
  - `GET /api/task-registration` shows the form when the passphrase was set, its fingerprint and recent wrong attempts, and each registration records the fingerprint.
  - **`scripts/set-owner-task-passphrase.ts`** installs a verifier and echoes its fingerprint. **`deploy/set-owner-task-passphrase.sh`** makes the verifier on the owner's computer and checks that fingerprint; the owner's deploy runs the merged copy.
- **`lib/store.ts`.** Registration markers (event ids, owner-session locator) are refused on every path except `appendOwnerTaskRegistration` (first review, P0). A registered worker's source, outcome, contract and reconciliation are refused on every other path too (second review, P1).
- **`lib/owner-task-source.ts`.** A registered task is under the current supervision rules from the start, keyed on the worker's registration, in the store (checkpoints, completion claims) and the terminal comparator (both reviews' P1).
- **`lib/projection.ts`, `lib/owner-view.ts`, the console and worker page.** A registered task shows as "Registered · waiting for its supervisor" until the first owner action or SUPERVISOR record, not as missing telemetry or as waiting for the owner. The worker page marks the goal and limits as typed on the form. The enrollment change is withdrawn: a task joins the fleet watch when its worker publishes a work queue.
- **`app/api/workers/route.ts` POST.** Owner session only, with same-origin and CSRF; the owner bearer gets 403. It relays the body unread.
- **`daemon/server.ts` POST `/workers`.** Owner producer and the passphrase; invalid JSON is a 400 and never logged.
- **UI.** `app/workers/new/page.tsx`, `components/RegisterTask.tsx` (passphrase field, the standard lines shown), a dashboard header link, styles.
- **README.** "Registering a task in the owner's own words", with what the check covers and what it doesn't.
- **Tests.** `tests/owner-task-registration.test.ts`, 11 tests; `tests/owner-task-registration-daemon.test.ts`, a real daemon; `tests/test_mission_control_owner_task_passphrase_setup.py`, the owner's setup script through a pseudo-terminal. Twenty-three mutations, each failing a test (see Evidence).
