## Completed

- Scheduler (`lib/provider-submission-authority.mjs`): `closeAmbiguousAdmission` closes one admission that is open or restart-ambiguous and has no boundary, abort, or outcome evidence, on the owner's decision to discard it (`OWNER_DISCARDED_NO_REPLAY`) or the owner's attestation that it was sent (`OWNER_ATTESTED_SUBMITTED_NO_REPLAY`). Only the admitting relay may close it, only for the exact admitted request and body, with a decision no more than 30 s in Mission Control's future, and only a full minimum interval plus 30 s after the later of the admission's expiry and that decision; earlier calls get `SUBMISSION_AMBIGUITY_CLOSURE_TOO_EARLY` with `closableAt`. The relay records the decision while holding its lock, so any click of the send happened before it. The admission becomes `AMBIGUOUS_CLOSED_NO_REPLAY` with its closure record; its queue item becomes the terminal `CLOSED_AMBIGUOUS_NO_REPLAY`, so its queue key can never be admitted again. The pacing boundary is left as recorded. A repeat of the same decision is idempotent; a different decision is refused.
- A boundary that arrives later for a closed admission is recorded as `AMBIGUOUS_EXPIRED_AT_BOUNDARY` (crossed without authority), advances the pacing boundary if later, and sets a safety halt unless one is already set.
- A closed ambiguous journal send still counts toward the journal allowance on every UTC day its admission window touches.
- Persisted state: closures are schema-checked on every load; a closure record on another status, a missing or altered closure, a closure time too soon after its decision or expiry, boundary evidence on a closed admission, or a queue terminal time that differs from the closure time all fail closed.
- Mission Control: daemon route and the authenticated API allowlist accept `admissions/close-ambiguous` from a bound relay collector; the ledger records `AMBIGUOUS_ADMISSION_CLOSED_NO_REPLAY` with the closure's decision, prior status, and times.
- Relay: `resolve <route-key> discard` and `submitted` also close Mission Control's admission for that route's exact send (by the admission the route recorded, otherwise Mission Control's open admission, which Mission Control matches to the route's request and body). The command waits once, up to five minutes, when Mission Control reports `closableAt`; re-running it on a resolved route repeats only the Mission Control step with the same recorded decision; it exits non-zero while Mission Control may still hold this send or another send open. `retry` and a started generation are unchanged.
