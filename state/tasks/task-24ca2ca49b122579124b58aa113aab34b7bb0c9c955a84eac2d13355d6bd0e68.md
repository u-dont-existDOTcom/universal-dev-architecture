  - A registered task's decision boundary falls back to its registered contract, so the first question can be asked. Other workers keep the old rule.
  - The first question's requested decision: the first bounded step within the owner's allowed scope and must-never limits, native ChatGPT Work only for collection Chat can't do reliably and only where the allowed scope includes it, and no spending, publishing or weakening of the contract.
