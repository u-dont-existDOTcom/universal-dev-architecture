## Candidate adaptation

### Method necessity / manufactured prerequisite

At consequential method commitments, separate:

- owner outcome;
- genuine constraints and their sources;
- proposed method and its origin;
- what specifically fails if the method is removed/replaced;
- strongest materially simpler alternative;
- evidence that rules the alternative out;
- ordinary-case specimen and difficult counterexample;
- necessity state: `ESTABLISHED | UNRESOLVED | NOT_NECESSARY`.

An assistant hypothesis must not acquire owner authority through repetition, hashes, green tests, downstream contracts, or sunk cost. `UNRESOLVED` permits a bounded reversible discriminating experiment, not architecture hardening.

This check is triggered by material changes in product meaning, judgment authority, evidence semantics, owner/user burden, costly dependencies, or long-lived architecture. It is not for routine reversible implementation details.

### Guidance activation provenance

A rule being present in GitHub is not proof it affected the current task. The existing Active Lesson Contract therefore records how current universal/project guidance actually entered the reasoning path and distinguishes `ACTIVE`, `NOT_ACTIVATED`, and `STALE`.

Valid routes can include a current project bootstrap, current task/owner activation, or an authenticated supervision route. The goal is truthful protection claims, not mandatory UDA adoption by every repository.

### Ordinary case before abstraction

At material forks, use a small end-to-end specimen early. If a rule blocks an ordinary supported case, compare uncertainty about the claim, a wrong subtype/model, and a wrong higher-level strategy before adding more schema or aftercare.

### Existing assurance plane

A material assistant-inferred prerequisite that survives until the owner has to discover it belongs in the existing Mission Control supervision-escape assurance plane. Do not build recursive meta-supervision. A repeated equivalent escape after a verified repair is evidence that the repair failed.

### Owner-facing method-fork card

At a genuine consequential method fork, surface one compact card: what the owner asked for, the additional assistant assumption, why it matters, the simplest live alternative, evidence of necessity, one concrete ordinary case, the recommended next action, and whether owner action is actually required.

Default owner action is `NONE` when a cheap reversible probe can resolve uncertainty. Routine implementation work should not generate the card.
