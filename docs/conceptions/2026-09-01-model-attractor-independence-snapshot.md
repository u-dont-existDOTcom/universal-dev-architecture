# Independent conception snapshot: escaping shared model attractors

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: PRESERVED BEFORE EXISTING-WORK SYNTHESIS
Owner: Joel Rosenblum
Scope: Somatic Introduction humanization experiment and reusable creative-generation supervision

## Problem

Repeated attempts to humanize prose converge on the same AI-shaped structural attractor even when the wording, prompt packet, context window, writer identity, or sentence boundary changes. The visible symptoms include balanced paradoxes, polished conceptual compression, semantic orbiting, tidy closure, and a final takeaway that the local prose did not earn.

## Owner observation that must not be softened

Using another model is not the proposed solution. The Authorial Graph experiment already tried a Claude/GPT division: Claude tended to congratulate the work, GPT tended to criticize it, the models argued, and the article did not advance. Two models do not fix a failure produced by the overall interaction structure or by priors shared across aligned language models.

## Candidate causal mechanism

The failure is plausibly generated by a combination of:

1. a shared instruction-tuned, reflective-expository prior across model families;
2. prompt and rubric structure that narrows the reachable output manifold;
3. sequential critique/refinement that makes the current candidate the anchor for the next candidate;
4. dense communication among writer, critic, and supervisor roles, which spreads one semantic orbit through the whole system;
5. authority and completion pressure that reward defensible explanation, balance, and closure rather than locally alive prose;
6. a Codex-mediated chat chain that paraphrases or compresses reasoning as it moves between owner, supervisor, and worker.

## State-independence hypothesis

Fresh state may help only when it is real and mechanically enforced. Opening another chat or naming another role is not sufficient if the same packet, critique language, archive, or inherited memory reconstructs the same attractor.

n8n could be useful as an orchestration control because it can create discrete executions with explicit inputs and outputs. Hermes could be useful as an isolated runner because it supports separate profiles and subagents. Neither is presumed to improve prose. Their value must be tested while holding model, prompt, candidate budget, and evaluator constant.

## Candidate insight

Replace debate and serial self-refinement with an **independent quality-diversity search**:

- generate candidates in physically and informationally isolated executions;
- use the same model family across experimental arms initially;
- give each execution a minimal positive prompt plus one precommitted structural behavior cell;
- never show a writer previous candidates, critic language, detector results, counters, or consequences;
- archive candidates by structural descriptors rather than by superficial wording;
- prefer coverage of different behavior cells before refining any one cell;
- evaluate candidates blind and freeze the literal verdict before reading state or consequences;
- do not let the evaluator rewrite the candidate;
- stop the generative lane after a bounded failure budget rather than adding another prohibition.

## Constraints

- Preserve owner meaning and article claims.
- Do not introduce unrelated human donor prose.
- Do not optimize against detector tokens or use a detector as the primary judge.
- Do not use multi-model debate as the default escape mechanism.
- Do not allow Codex, n8n, or Hermes to author semantic verdicts.
- Keep exact candidate, prompt, model/runtime identity, timestamps, and provenance receipts.
- Treat unknown runtime or message provenance as unverified.
- If no bounded cell produces a genuine editorial pass, abandon generative reconstruction as the primary lane and switch to owner-speech or owner-authored source transformation.

## Falsifiers

This conception is weakened if a controlled experiment shows that:

- debate or serial refinement reliably raises blind editorial pass rate without semantic drift;
- orchestration isolation produces no increase in structural coverage or editorial pass rate;
- the behavior-cell archive merely creates cosmetic variation around the same underlying closure;
- blind evaluators cannot agree above chance on the target failure family;
- non-generative owner-source transformation is not materially better than the generative arms.
