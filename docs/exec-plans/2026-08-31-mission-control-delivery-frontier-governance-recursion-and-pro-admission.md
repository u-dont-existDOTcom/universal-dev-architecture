## 1. Controlling owner correction

Two current projects exposed the same remaining supervision failure through different surface symptoms.

### AskRigor

The worker produced a strong formal research-frontier repository, partial-corpus semantics, public operation 22, many synthetic Community Forum contracts, seven migrations, and a 30-case hostile matrix. Those are useful. But the broader owner outcome is a usable evidence/community/recruitment product. There is still no demonstrated visible end-to-end gap-to-contribution-to-research loop, real forum pilot, or deployed recruitment mechanism. The system could keep adding architecture and synthetic hostile coverage indefinitely while remaining locally green.

### Human Design / AstroHD

The worker produced a substantial product and scientific-governance foundation, then entered repeated governance and Pro-checkpoint recursion: thousands of lines of schemas, manifests, method scans, acceptance matrices, dossiers, and checkpoint packets, often with no production-source change and no human-authored construct, reliability result, mapping result, birth-time recovery result, or relationship-validation result. The latest continuation also remained local rather than becoming a durable reviewable GitHub boundary.

The governing correction is:

> **Mission Control must supervise the next direct delivery/evidence frontier, not merely approve another internally valid supporting-work child. Architecture, governance, tests, synthetic fixtures, reviews, and supervision are budgeted enabling work. Once the enabling foundation is sufficient, another same-family support cycle is unauthorized unless it is tied to a concrete missing capability discovered by an attempted vertical slice or direct evidence boundary.**

And:

> **Extra High is the default reasoning surface. Pro is admitted for a particular high-consequence decision, not inherited because a task already has a Pro chat or has historically used Pro.**

And:

> **Meaningful work must become durable and independently reviewable at the next safe checkpoint. A large local-only branch is not durable progress unless a current privacy/security reason prevents publication and a sanitized hash-bound receipt is pushed instead.**

## 2. Role boundary

This document is the reasoning decision. Codex implements it mechanically.

Codex must not:

- decide the owner’s real product or scientific outcome;
- choose the next vertical slice or human-evidence design;
- classify a live project’s scientific validity;
- decide that governance or synthetic work is sufficient;
- invent a Pro-admission rationale;
- recommend among consequential owner choices;
- contact participants or launch a pilot;
- merge or deploy project repositories merely because this universal architecture changes.

Extra High owns ordinary project reasoning, root-gap analysis, vertical-slice design, recommendation, review, and execution-directive composition. Pro is used only through the admission gate below. Codex validates required fields and executes the chat-authored plan.

## 3. Bounded existing-work scan and disposition

Do not invent a new general project-management doctrine. Compose established controls:

- **Lean value-stream thinking:** distinguish customer/user value from local activity and remove work that does not advance the value stream. See Lean Enterprise Institute, “Lean Thinking and Practice.”
- **Evidence-Based Management:** measure progress toward outcomes/value under uncertainty rather than equating output volume with success. See Scrum.org, *Evidence-Based Management Guide*.
- **Technology/readiness levels:** architecture, laboratory proof, integrated prototype, relevant-environment demonstration, and operational use are different maturity classes. See NASA Technology Readiness Levels.
- **End-to-end service and user research practice:** a prototype or beta intended for evaluation must work end-to-end; technical tests do not replace usability or real-task evidence. See GOV.UK Service Manual guidance on whole services, prototypes, and user research.
- **Goal/claim-to-evidence traceability already present in this repository:** lower-level evidence cannot silently satisfy a higher-level owner outcome.

Disposition: **ADAPT + COMPOSE**.

Reusable established core:

```text
value/outcome defined independently of activity
bounded work in progress
explicit maturity/evidence class
direct end-to-end demonstration
measurement and feedback
stop/replan when the intended result does not arrive
```

Project-specific adaptation:

```text
chat-authored root delivery frontier
machine-checkable evidence-capability floor
supporting-work and supervision budgets
anti-governance-recursion kill switch
canonical GitHub durability
hard Extra-High/Pro admission routing
real-failure fixtures from AskRigor and Human Design
```

## 5. Evidence capability must match the claim

Every owner requirement must declare the minimum evidence capability that could satisfy it. Every evidence artifact declares what it can support.

Required general capability classes:

```text
ARCHITECTURE_OR_ARGUMENT
DETERMINISTIC_CONTRACT
SYNTHETIC_COMPONENT
SYNTHETIC_INTEGRATION
END_TO_END_SYNTHETIC_PRODUCT
LIVE_OPERATIONAL_NONHUMAN
REAL_WORLD_DEVELOPMENT
INDEPENDENT_REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION
RELEASED_OPERATIONAL_OUTCOME
```

These are capability labels, not a universal scalar of quality. A task may define additional domain-specific classes and an explicit satisfaction matrix.

Rules:

- tests of a schema support deterministic-contract claims;
- synthetic oracle/noise tests support engineering recoverability, not human predictive validity;
- a local synthetic forum supports integration behavior, not a usable or safe real forum;
- a deployed endpoint supports operational availability, not scientific correctness;
- a Pro verdict supports a judgment record, not the underlying outcome;
- a neutral dossier supports decision preparation, not a decision;
- a draft PR supports durability/reviewability, not release.

If the evidence capability is below the owner requirement’s declared floor:

```text
evidence_satisfaction = INELIGIBLE_FOR_REQUIREMENT
```

Required findings:

```text
EVIDENCE_CAPABILITY_MISMATCH
SYNTHETIC_EVIDENCE_SUBSTITUTED_FOR_REAL_WORLD_CLAIM
ARCHITECTURE_SUBSTITUTED_FOR_USABLE_PRODUCT
REVIEW_APPROVAL_SUBSTITUTED_FOR_OUTCOME
```

Classify every material child cycle as one primary type:


Supporting work includes enablement, safety, governance, supervision, tooling, and some evidence acquisition. It may be essential. It does not become direct progress through volume.

Every strategy family must set a support budget since the last direct evidence boundary:

```text
maximumSupportCycles
maximumGovernanceCycles
maximumSupervisionReviews
maximumElapsedSecondsWithoutFrontierEvidence
maximumFilesOrArtifactsWhenMaterial

```

Universal defaults may be conservative and project-configurable; they must not be absent.

Before authorizing another support-only child, the reasoning decision must state:

```text
named root delivery frontier
specific missing capability this child supplies
why the capability is not already present
how completion changes the next executable frontier
why a vertical-slice attempt cannot or should not happen first
support budget remaining
```

Reject another support-only cycle when:

- the same governance/supervision family has completed without root evidence;
- the child produces another schema, matrix, dossier, method scan, or hostile corpus without a newly observed blocker from an attempted frontier;
- the owner outcome needs an end-to-end product demonstration but work remains component-local;
- the owner outcome needs real-world evidence but work remains synthetic;
- another review is proposed only because the prior review authorized one child at a time;
- the next child has no direct evidence or decision boundary due at completion.

Required states/findings:

```text
SUPPORTING_WORK_WITHIN_BUDGET
```


unless a reasoning chat authorizes a materially different frontier under the existing `NO_VALID_STRATEGY` and failed-strategy-lineage rules.

## 7. Architecture-to-vertical-slice transition

When an architecture/contract/synthetic foundation is accepted, the default next frontier is the smallest user-visible or evidence-bearing end-to-end slice—not another generalized subsystem.

A valid vertical slice:

- begins at an actual user/researcher/operator need;
- crosses every required system boundary;
- ends in a visible result or direct evidence artifact;
- is small enough to complete under one bounded strategy cycle;
- has a hard failure condition;
- uses existing architecture before proposing more architecture.

New generalized architecture is permitted only when an attempted vertical slice exposes a concrete missing capability that cannot be safely solved locally.

Required finding:

```text
VERTICAL_SLICE_DEFERRED_WITHOUT_CAUSE
```

For real-user or human-evidence work, owner/privacy/ethics authorization remains mandatory. If that gate is not yet open, the correct frontier is:

```text
CONSOLIDATED_OWNER_GATE_DUE
```

The worker prepares one recommended bounded proposal and stops before contact. It must not fill the waiting period with indefinite governance expansion.

## 8. Decision convergence; prohibit neutral-dossier recursion

Exploration may initially produce a neutral option inventory. It cannot remain neutral indefinitely.

Required decision states:

```text
EXPLORING
RECOMMENDATION_DUE
OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED
DECIDED
NO_VALID_STRATEGY
```

After one complete decision inventory and one reasoning review, the next packet must normally contain:

```text
recommended option
why it best advances the owner outcome
viable fallback(s)

exact authority requested

```

A second neutral dossier with no recommendation requires a specific reason why evidence is genuinely insufficient and what bounded evidence would resolve it.

Required finding:

```text
DECISION_DEFERRAL_LOOP
```

Consolidate related owner choices into one decision packet when they govern the same pilot/release boundary. Do not make the owner answer a long chain of preventable micro-decisions.

## 10. Canonical durability and local-only work

At every meaningful checkpoint, nonprivate work must be pushed to the canonical repository and represented by a branch/PR or exact immutable commit reference.

Each task must configure a maximum local-only horizon in one or more of:

```text
elapsed time
completed cycles
commit count
reasoning reviews
```

When exceeded:

```text
LOCAL_ONLY_WORK_OVERDUE
```

Required response:

1. stop new substantive execution;
2. secret/privacy scan;
3. push the safe branch and open/update a draft PR;
4. for content that cannot enter GitHub, preserve it privately and push a sanitized hash-bound receipt identifying excluded classes/paths;
5. update task-local current state so it outranks stale global execution state;
6. resume only after the exact durable boundary is available to the reasoning chat.

Do not leave an entire large branch unpushed because one artifact is private.

A worker summary or chat transcript is not a substitute for canonical durability.

## 11. Hard Pro-admission gate

Reasoning defaults to:

```text

```

```text
MANDATED_HIGH_CONSEQUENCE_DOMAIN
EXTRA_HIGH_ESCALATION
INDEPENDENT_HIGH_CONSEQUENCE_ADJUDICATION
```

Required fields:

```text
proAdmissionId

admissionBasis
owner/task domain
exact decision that can change
reviewed evidence boundary
why Extra High is insufficient
consequence if wrong
expected marginal value of Pro
prior Extra High decision/ref when applicable
independence required
owner mandate ref when applicable
expiry / one-decision scope
```

Rules:

- an existing Pro chat, historical Pro use, task label, or phrase such as “existing Pro supervisor” is not admission;
- ordinary repository reading, product planning, architecture, article writing, routine progress review, pilot drafting, diff review, implementation review, and packet construction use Extra High;
- material therapy semantics/safety and AskRigor methodological or conclusion-validity adjudication normally justify Pro;
- Human Design mapping validity, leakage/circularity disputes, contamination/adjudication semantics, and final high-consequence measurement freezes may justify Pro;
- do not require a ceremonial Extra High hop when Pro is explicitly mandated;
- when Pro is merely allowed and unavailable, fall back to Extra High;
- when Pro is required and unavailable, park only the affected reasoning boundary;
- Pro calls are counted by decision, not by messages/polls.

After each Pro call, record:

```text
what decision changed
whether Extra High had already supplied an adequate answer
whether the call was avoidable
```

Required findings:

```text
PRO_USED_WITHOUT_ADMISSION
PRO_ADMISSION_INCOMPLETE
PRO_ROUTING_INHERITED_FROM_EXISTING_CHAT
AVOIDABLE_PRO_USAGE
PRO_REVIEW_RECURSION
```
